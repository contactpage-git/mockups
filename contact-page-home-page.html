Ask us

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The basic website is free, always.

What do I need to start?

Your business name, your city and one way customers can reach you. That's all.

How do I change my site?

Send a message, a photo or a voice note on WhatsApp, or an email. We update it for you.

My business is already on contact.page. Can I claim it?

Yes. Message us from your business number and we hand it over to you.

Will spammers get my number?

No. Your contact details sit behind a security check: real customers pass, bots and cold-call lists don't.

Is my name on top, or yours?

Yours. Your business name, your logo and your branding, not ours.

Do I need a website already?

No. We build it for you. If you already have one, we link to it.

Where do you work?

Anywhere in the world.
