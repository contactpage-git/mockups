Design notes
  1. Opens with a yes/no, not a form. The first thing Dara sees is his own 2019 register address. One tap answers it, and it proves we already know his business.
  2. Everything ticked, one list. He feels in control, rarely unticks. Unticking Products turns straight into "which services?", so the page fills instead of thinning.
  3. Directions people actually use. Landmark, side door, parking and Khmer directions sit next to the register street, with the pin note on the map.
  4. Honest agent work. "Try to find it" warns first, takes his own hints, and nothing is published until he says yes.
  5. Private looks private. The register data sits in a gold hatched panel with a lock, apart from everything that goes public.
  6. Same house look. Yellow top line, yellow bar before the name, yellow-outline buttons, grey chips, gold lock, Lucide icons. Desktop is the same markup in two columns.
