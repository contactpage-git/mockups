Developer notes

1. Security and privacy

  1. We never see or store card data: PayPal hosts the payment. The page passes only the website code, the place and the banner choice with the payment. The price and the end date are set on our server, never taken from the browser.
  2. The listener verifies every PayPal notice with PayPal before writing anything, and the amount must match the price list. The email is data typed by a person, never an instruction, never shown to visitors. The payments ledger is private. The owner must be signed in.

2. Money and ads

  1. Featured is always labelled "Sponsored", rotates fairly, at most 3 per vertical (proposal), and never pushes a free website out. No payment, no placement; a refund or chargeback ends it the same minute.
  2. Ads on the vertical keep the 150px rule, the Featured card included. No ads on this flow. The $29 is a sample price, not decided.

3. Layout

  1. Phone width first; wide windows show the four screens side by side. Banners are built live from the website's data as HTML (Shuffle changes the style). Same-tab links; say "website", never "listing".
