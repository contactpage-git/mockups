Questions about Office space for rent in Phnom Penh

Can I view an office before I decide, and when?

Most buildings arrange viewings in office hours. Yellow Tower shows offices Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00.

Which Phnom Penh offices have parking?

Yellow Tower has 900 m² of parking on site, and City Tower in Toul Kork has two basement levels for cars and motorbikes. Ask each building about parking, because it differs.

Can I rent a smaller part of a big floor?

Often, in the larger towers. At Vattanac Capital, floors of 1,461 to 1,769 m² can be split into suites from 100 m².

Do I have to furnish the office myself?

Not always. The Helix Office offers furnished, semi-furnished and bare units, and Regus Business Centre in Canadia Tower offers furnished serviced offices.

Is electricity included in the rent?

It depends on the operator. The Helix Office includes electricity at no added cost; ask each building what its rent includes.

Before you choose

Which part of Phnom Penh should I look in?

Daun Penh and the Preah Monivong Blvd spine hold the Grade A towers and the classic corporate addresses, with 7 Makara on the same boulevard. Toul Kork has newer buildings with parking. Chroy Changvar, across the river, is calm and green. Pick the district that is easiest for your team and your clients to reach.

Serviced, semi-serviced or bare?

A serviced office comes furnished with reception and meeting rooms on short terms. A semi-serviced office shares some services, and The Helix Office offers furnished, semi-furnished and bare units. A bare unit suits a team that wants to build its own space.

What should I compare?

Start with the type of office, the size available, whether it is furnished, the parking, what the rent includes and the viewing times. Ask each building for the details, because they differ from one to the next.

Words you may meet

Serviced office
A furnished office with reception, meeting rooms and short terms, with the rent all-in. Business centres such as Regus offer these.
Semi-serviced office
Some services are shared, such as meeting rooms and utilities, with shorter terms than a normal lease.
Bare or shell
An empty unit: the tenant builds the inside.
Fit-out
Building the inside of an office: walls, cabling and furniture.
Grade A, B, C
The market's shorthand for building quality and location; Grade A is the top towers.
Floor plate
The usable area of one whole floor.
Service charge
A monthly fee for shared areas and building services, on top of the rent.
Coworking
A desk or a small room in a shared space, rented by the month.
