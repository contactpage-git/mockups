  1. OWNER SCREEN MOCKUP v1, 2026-10-06, Page Bot. Spec: CONTACT/kb/data-pool-design-2026-10-06.md, "Owner layers" 1-7.
What it is: the owner's own screen behind the security door (signed in). Mockup only, nothing here is wired to production.
Sample data: Mekong Bean Coffee is INVENTED (not from the MOC crawl). Phone +855 12 000 000 and *.example emails are fake.
ORDER (mobile, one column):
 greeting = interview question 1 from the MOC record (layer 6 + 1) -> your contact details (full values, owner only)
 -> what your page shows: field checklist, all ticked by default, Products unticked as the example (layer 7)
 -> address and directions with the extra direction fields (layer 6) -> missing details with "Try to find it" (layer 4)
 -> announcements composer (archive-only comms) -> FAQ from the owner -> refresh my page (layer 2)
 -> private panel: deep register data, owner-pass (layer 1).
DESKTOP (container 920px+): two columns. Main: greeting, address, missing details, announcements, FAQ.
 Side: contact details, checklist, refresh, private panel. Same markup, CSS container queries; the "Phone" toggle in the
 mockup bar puts the same screen in a 390px phone frame.
RULES APPLIED: design package colours (#FFC400 accents, gold lock #E0A500, ink #161616, lines #e9e9e9, light only); buttons are
 the yellow outline with the highlight stroke; chips per the chip rule; Lucide/Simple Icons from CONTACT/design/icons, no emoji;
 the 4px yellow top line and the yellow bar before the name (frozen). Words to owners: "your page", never "listing"
 (so the button reads "Refresh my page"). Comms lines (announcements) are archive-only; profile fields just overwrite.
OPEN FOR KIM: (1) Khmer lines are a draft and need a native check. (2) The register record normally carries the registration
 number; the empty-field example uses it because the brief asked for it, a real owner would more likely see e.g. the patent
 tax or VAT certificate number empty. (3) Owners still change everything by WhatsApp too: every card keeps the agent route.

  2. Mockup v1 · Page Bot · 6 Oct 2026 · sample data, invented business (no visible notes, Kim 2026-10-07)
  3. TOP BAR. Logo, the business name (desktop), link to the public page, owner avatar.
  4. SECURITY DOOR STRIP. Tells the owner why he sees full values: he is signed in and nobody else sees this screen.
  5. IDENTITY. Same kicker and yellow bar as the public page, so the owner recognises his own page.
  6. 1. GREETING = INTERVIEW QUESTION 1 (layers 6 + 1). Opens with his own register record: an easy yes/no, and proof we
already know his business. IF no register match: open with the most valuable empty field instead. Yes = confirm and
go to question 2; Fix it = the address fields below open for editing. Progress = questions answered, not % of fields.
  7. 3. ADDRESS AND DIRECTIONS (layer 6). Street from the register; the extra fields help people actually find the door.
Each field overwrites (profile field, no history). IF a field is empty: it shows as a grey "Add" row and becomes an
interview question. Map preview is drawn by us, never an embed (D8).
  8. 4. MISSING DETAILS with "Try to find it" (layer 4). Each empty field has a small button. Tapped: the warning (no
guarantee) and an optional box for his own sources, then Go. The result comes back as a message, never auto-published:
he confirms it first. Row 1 shows the open state, row 2 the default.
  9. 7a. ANNOUNCEMENTS composer. A comms line: archive-only, never edited (rule 2026-10-06). Type chips, how long it shows,
Khmer copy on/off. IF none posted: the block is hidden on the public page, the composer stays here.
  10. 7b. FAQ FROM THE OWNER. His own words, labelled as his. Suggestions come from his trade (layer 3). On the public page
it joins the FAQ block (2+ real answers rule) and its JSON-LD.
  11. CONTACT DETAILS, full values (owner's private screen). Visitors only ever see them after the security check.
  12. 2. FIELD CHECKLIST (layer 7). Every section in one list, ALL ticked by default. Untick = hidden from the page and the
interview, never deleted, tick it back any time. Products unticked as the example, with the nudge that turns
"no products" into "which services?".
  13. 5. REFRESH MY PAGE (layer 2). Agents re-pull and enrich; about a day; we message him when done. IF a refresh is
running: the button turns into "Refreshing, started 21:40" and is off until it ends.
  14. 6. PRIVATE PANEL (layer 1, owner-pass). Deep register data only the verified owner sees; never on the public page,
never in the JSON-LD, never sent to visitors or AIs. Shareholders and full filings need his own MOC login.
