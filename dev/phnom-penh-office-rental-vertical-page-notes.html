  3. WHY IT IS THIS WAY (reasons and lessons)
- The vertical is the traffic page: one city, one category, real businesses, so it can rank for the long-tail search and feed
  visitors into listings (mini-vertical strategy). Page 1 of the standard set: vertical -> listing -> security -> contact details.
- Cards are real businesses, every fact sourced; a business reported closed was dropped (Kim 2026-10-07, N33). Ranked by
  verification count once those counts are stored (N12).
- Card buttons go straight to each business's security page (N17): the visitor wants the contact, the gate is a mini listing.
- SEO head: title and description differ from the H1 and carry the long-tail words; breadcrumb and item list in JSON-LD.
- Same rules as the listing: no prices (N31), no website (N22), no ratings (C10), masked numbers only, no jump links.
- Cards without a real photo get a static map image, never a live map embed (Kim 2026-10-07).
  4. BREADCRUMB (Kim 2026-10-07: the kicker repeated the H1, weak for SEO). A real nav with links, BreadcrumbList JSON-LD in
the head mirrors it. Cambodia = the live Cambodia portal today (www.contact.page; becomes cambodia.contact.page only if
Kim approves that move, site-architecture.md). Phnom Penh: no city page exists yet, so the mockup links the Phnom Penh
preview index in the repo (production: the Phnom Penh city page, route still to decide). Last crumb = this page, not a link.
  5. H1 (one only) with the frozen yellow line: the main search phrase. Subline = count + value, plain text (count is the mockup
count; production prints the real number of cards). Intro: two sentences of natural long-tail words (districts, serviced /
private / small office, co-working); no prices, no claims about any business, no keyword lists.
  6. AD #1, 300x250 fixed, right after the identity (design-evidence rule 1; "the page earns its ads", Kim 2026-10-05).
The first card comes after it with content in between, never a button right under the ad.
  7. CARDS ("Top standouts", Kim 2026-10-07: vertical cards are editorial teasers, a standout angle plus real highlights, not
listing fields). Yellow Tower is card 1; cards 2-6 are REAL Phnom Penh office-rental businesses (Kim 2026-10-07,
conflicts N33). Every fact has a source (live contact.page page, the business's own site, or a public reference; list
in design-state.md); a fact without a source is left out. No ratings yet: none found from a source we can cite (C10
still holds until a sourced rating exists). No photos of cards 2-6 in the repo, so a grey placeholder.
Each card (Kim 2026-10-07): full-width 3:2 photo on top (object-fit cover, rounded top corners; no real photo =
neutral placeholder with the category icon, never a fake photo), small yellow rank badge over the photo's top-right
corner; below: name (links to the business's page), area, angle line, 1-2 sentences of highlights, tags (business
activities from the live page), masked number, lock button to the security page (V5; N17: straight to the gate,
/contact-details?from=vertical on live sites). Only card 1 has the bold yellow-underline button; cards 2-6 use the
light one (outline + lock, normal weight, no thick underline; Kim 2026-10-07). Order 2-6 is not a verified ranking
yet (D007). No prices (N31), no website (N22), numbers masked, no digits in source.
  8. MASKED NUMBERS (Kim 2026-10-07): show all but the last 3 digits ("+855 12 869 ***"), never more hidden. The repo holds
no raw phone digits, so masks are placeholders ({{YT_PHONE_MASKED}}, {{MASK_KH...}}) filled at publish from the box-only
values file; other businesses' numbers come from their live <id>.contact.page listings.
