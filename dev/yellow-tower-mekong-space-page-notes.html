  2. WHY IT IS THIS WAY
- One address and one map pin for the building and everything inside it: the listing says where inside ("floor not stated") instead of repeating a second address.
- The line "Located in Yellow Tower" is the link between the two pages, so visitors and search engines see the building and what is inside it.
- Only public facts from the business's own website; floor and sizes are shown only where it states them.
