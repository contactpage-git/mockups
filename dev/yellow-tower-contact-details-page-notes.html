  3. WHY IT IS THIS WAY (reasons and lessons)
- This page is the payoff after the check: the real contact lines, noindex, never cached for everyone (N14).
- Confirmed lines first, unconfirmed below with a "did it work?" vote: visitors make the data better, and a number that fails
  jumps the refresh queue. No stars or ratings, only facts about a number (C10).
- Contact lines are add-only: old ones are archived with their date, never deleted (Kim 2026-10-06 data rule).
- The role from the security page ("the owner or staff") turns on the owner path here; owners act through our agent.
- No AdSense next to contact buttons; our house ad instead. No website row until a link check exists (N22).
