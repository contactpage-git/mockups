All notes, summary first, then in page order

  1. WHATSAPP HANDLE: @page.me (Kim 2026-10-07, claimed live in WhatsApp the same evening)

OUR REASON, in full:
1. The domain-suffix assumption was wrong. The team believed ".me is a real TLD, WhatsApp will block it" (same wall as @contact.page). Kim tested it live — WhatsApp accepted @page.me with no block. Lesson: platform rules get tested in the dialog, never asserted from memory.
2. It reads like a premium domain name. Short, ownable, memorable — an asset, not just a username. (Note: page.me the domain belongs to LinkedIn; the handle is independent of that.)
3. "Page me!" is commandable and alive. Six characters plus the dot. It is what you shout across the shop, and it doubles as the site's CTA language without changing a word.
4. The roundtable ran 24 rounds on this. @atservice (Grok/Z.ai pick, "I am Page, at your service") survived every kill test and was the reasoned winner. Kim chose @page.me anyway — gut over rubrics, his call, his account. The "pague-me = pay me" scam-DM worry (Z.ai) was dropped: never observed in the wild.
5. Email stays newcustomers@contact.page for now (separate decision; MX records).
  2. HOME PAGE SOURCE: CONTACT/pages/home/index.html (Claude design conversation). The mockup is a copy; the design chat is the authority on presentation (design-sync skill). Do not restyle from the wiki alone.
  3. LINK CONTRACT: all WhatsApp links use wa.me/<handle> with tagged prefilled ?text= messages ([HOME|signup], [HOME|find], ref home plus, ref home featured). Test on a phone before launch; if WhatsApp does not open the chat, use the number instead.
