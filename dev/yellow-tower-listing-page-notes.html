AD PLAN (growth within AdSense policy)
- Ad #1 right after the identity: the most-seen spot on the page, with the category and place words around it so the ads
  match what the visitor came for.
- The gap after Ad #1 is now a compact facts strip with no heading (about 110px, nothing tappable): the contact box comes
  sooner, but never right against the ad. Google counts buttons near ads as an accidental-click risk; it would put the whole
  account at risk, which is the one thing survival can't afford.
- Ad #mid between Photos and About, Ad #2 at the bottom: three units on a long page, each with content on both sides,
  lower two lazy-loaded.
- Never: ads on the security gate, ads next to the contact or call buttons, two ads in a row, wording that invites clicks,
  styling a card to look like an ad or an ad to look like content.
- Measure: page RPM and contact reveals per session (design-evidence events) before and after; drop Ad #mid if reveals fall.
  2. DEVELOPER (audit 2026-10-07, not visible): PRODUCTION INDEXING RULES

ADS (item 8): AdSense script async and loaded lazily (after first interaction or when a slot nears the viewport); every

security page. Cloudflare Turnstile ONLY on the security page, nowhere else.
  3. WHY IT IS THIS WAY (reasons and lessons)
- The page earns its ads (survival first, Kim 2026-10-05/06): identity, then the first ad, then a gap of plain content, then
  the contact box. Google counts buttons next to ads as accidental-click bait, which can put the whole AdSense account at risk,
  so the contact button never sits against an ad. A third ad mid-page, lower ads lazy-loaded: more income within the rules.
- No jump links: they let people skip past the ads (design-evidence rule 3).
- No numbers on this page: "View contact details" goes to the security page, the only way to the numbers (C5, D33).
- For AI assistants and bots: the JSON-LD has no number, only contactPoint.url = the contact-details page, so an assistant
  answering "how do I call Yellow Tower?" gives its user that link to unlock (Kim 2026-09-21, kb/agent-back-channel.md).
- No prices and no offers: we don't collect prices; they show only when the owner adds them or a synced source keeps them
  updated (Kim 2026-10-06, N31). No currency anywhere (frozen rule).
- No website: too many dead sites end in 404 pages (N22). No video (frozen). No Google Maps: our own map page (N32).
- Owners change everything through our agent on WhatsApp or email, never forms: every block ends in a small grey link that
  opens the agent with a tagged message (Kim 2026-10-06).
- Photos: one box size (4:3 crop) with rounded corners, real photos from the owner's own site (Kim 2026-10-06).
- Section headers link to their own subpages; chips are tight and square (Kim's chip rule); icons only from the design package.
- Lessons: v15 left five old photo placeholders and a stray closing tag that pushed everything after Photos out of the phone
  frame until v21. Replace a block up to its own closing tag, then look at the page in the frame before publishing.
