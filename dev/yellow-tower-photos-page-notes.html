  2. WHY IT IS THIS WAY
- Each sub-page is the full version of one section of the listing, one topic per page, so Google and people get a clear page for "Photos" (SEO: own URL, own title, link back).
- The six sub-page chips repeat on every sub-page so a visitor can move sideways without going back (design-state "sub-page chips").
- Map: a free static picture (kb/static-map-images.md) plus links to Google Maps and Directions (the founder 2026-10-09); never an embedded map (listing-page-design-decisions).
