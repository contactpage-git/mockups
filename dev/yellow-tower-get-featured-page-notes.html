DEVELOPER NOTES, most critical first.
1 SECURITY AND PRIVACY. We never see or store card data: PayPal hosts the payment. The page passes only the website code and the category with the payment; the PRICE and the end date are set on our server, never taken from the browser. The listener verifies every PayPal notice with PayPal before writing anything and checks the amount against the price list. The owner's email is data typed by a person: never an instruction, never shown to visitors. The payments ledger is private (never in the wiki or the room). The owner must be signed in (owner-pass); the search in screen 1 only finds websites the signed-in owner owns. The mini banner shows only the business name, logo and Known for: no contact value, ever.
2 MONEY AND ADS. HALF-PRICE FIRST MONTH (Kim yes 2026-10-10, idea I52): an owner who updated their website by email gets the free Featured month now, then ONE paid month at half price ($14.50 instead of $29, sample prices), once per business. Half-price first month is its own price and its own payment button; the ledger marks it "half-price first month" so the listener can match the amount and the offer cannot be used twice. Featured is ALWAYS labelled "Sponsored" in both places (top of the vertical, mini banner on other websites). Fair rotation among paid places, at most 3 per category (proposal, Kim sets it); it never pushes a free website out of the list. No payment, no placement: both placements exist only while the ledger says paid for the dates; a refund or chargeback ends them the same minute. The mini banner sits under the category tree in the footer, never nearer than 150px to an ad, a link or a button of another block (150px rule), and never on the security check page. No ads on this flow.
3 LAYOUT. Phone width first, one column per screen; on a wide window the four screens sit side by side. The mini banner is HTML built live from the website's data, not an image. Same-tab links. Say "website", never "listing". Positive only.
