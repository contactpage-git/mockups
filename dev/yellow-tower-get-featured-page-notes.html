DEVELOPER NOTES, most critical first.
1 SECURITY AND PRIVACY. We never see or store card data: PayPal hosts the payment. The page passes only the website code, the place and the banner choice along with the payment; the PRICE and the end date are set on our server, never taken from the browser. The listener verifies every PayPal notice with PayPal before writing anything and checks the amount against the price list. The owner's email is data typed by a person: never an instruction, never shown to visitors. The payments ledger is private (never in the wiki or the room). The owner must be signed in (owner-pass) to reach this flow.
2 MONEY AND ADS. Featured is ALWAYS labelled "Sponsored", rotates fairly among paid places, at most 3 per vertical (proposal, Kim sets it), and never pushes a free website out of the list. No payment, no placement: a placement exists only while the ledger says paid for the dates. A refund or chargeback ends it the same minute. Ads on the vertical keep the 150px rule (never nearer than 150px to a link or button, the Featured card included). No ads on this flow.
3 LAYOUT. Phone width first, one column per screen; on a wide window the four screens sit side by side. Banners are built live from the website's data (name, logo, Known for) as HTML, not images; Shuffle changes the style. Same-tab links. Say "website", never "listing". Positive only.
