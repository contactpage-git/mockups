About Contact Page

Contact Page gives every local business a free website at your.contact.page, kept up to date by our AI page helper, just by chatting on WhatsApp or email.

We've done this for a long time

  • Since the 1990s: local business directories, from Cambodia's first Yellow Pages in 2001.
  • Since 2007: our business data licensed to Google for Google Maps.
  • 9 million business listings across Southeast Asia (2016), as PanPages, listed on Bursa Malaysia.
  • Directory technology that ran Yellow Pages and phone books in Asia and Africa, in English and French.
  • Our founders go way back: putting local businesses on the map since before smartphones.

Now the page works for you. The same job, done by AI: your site, your customers, no typing.
