Questions

How does billing work?

Plus is paid in advance, for 1 month or 1 year at a time. It does not renew automatically. Our payment partner handles the payment and sends your receipt by email.

Do I need to cancel?

No. Nothing renews automatically. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for, then your website moves to the free Basic plan. It stays online.

How do refunds work?

Every payment has a 30-day money-back guarantee. Use the link in your payment receipt or email hello@contact.page and you get a full refund to your original payment method.

Which countries can sign up?

Businesses in most countries. We accept cards and other local payment methods, and the right tax for your country is worked out at checkout. A few countries cannot be served because of payment and sanctions rules.

Is tax included in the price?

No. Prices exclude tax. VAT or sales tax for your country is added at checkout and shown before you pay.

Do I need a website already?

No. You need your business name, your city and one way customers can reach you. If your business is already on contact.page, you can claim it when you sign up.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade, switch between monthly and yearly, or go back to Basic at any time.
