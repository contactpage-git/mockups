FAQ

How much does office space cost at Yellow Tower?

Listed units: office space 375 a month, co-working 88 a month, a 500 m² private office (1st floor) 2,500 a month and a 450 m² private office (3rd floor) 2,700 a month. Private offices run 5 to 12 per m².

Is there a move-in offer?

Yes. The first month is rent-free when you rent direct.

When can I view a unit?

Viewings are Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00. Use the contact details above to book one.

Can I buy instead of rent?

Yes. Strata title is available for floors 1 to 3; price on request.

About Yellow Tower

The seven-story Yellow Tower is the greenest office space in Phnom Penh, wrapped in over 2,400 square meters of vertical garden that keeps the building cool and calm. Offices have high ceilings and mezzanines, natural light and high-speed internet, set on the Chroy Changva peninsula with parking and a view over the river.
