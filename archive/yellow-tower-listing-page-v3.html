About Yellow Tower

The seven-story Yellow Tower is the greenest office space in Phnom Penh, wrapped in over 2,400 square meters of vertical garden that keeps the building cool and calm. Offices have high ceilings and mezzanines, natural light and high-speed internet, set on the Chroy Changva peninsula with parking and a great view over the river.

FAQ

How much does office space cost at Yellow Tower?

Listed units run 375/mo for office space, 88/mo for co-working, 2,500/mo for a 500 m² private office (1st floor) and 2,700/mo for a 450 m² private office (3rd floor). Private offices are 5–12/m².

Is there a move-in offer?

Yes — the first month is rent-free when you rent direct.
