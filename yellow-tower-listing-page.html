FAQ

How big are the offices?

Full office floors are 450 to 500 m² (floors 1, 2, 3 and 5). Private offices of 20 to 40 m² are on floor 2.

About Yellow Tower

The seven-story Yellow Tower is the greenest office space in Phnom Penh, wrapped in over 2,400 square meters of vertical garden that keeps the building cool and calm. Offices have high ceilings and mezzanines, natural light and high-speed internet, set on the Chroy Changva peninsula with parking and a view over the river.
