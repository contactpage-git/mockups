    How do I change something?

    Tap a row and type. Every change is saved in this browser at once. Use Export JSON to keep a copy, Import JSON to load one, Reset to start again.

    What do Public, Behind the check, Only you and We fill mean?

    Public: shown on your page. Behind the check: shown only after a visitor passes the security check. Only you: never public. We fill: our agents fill it, you can read it.

    Can I hide a section?

    Yes. Untick "Show on my page" inside the section. It is hidden, never deleted, and you can tick it again. Export JSON lists it under hiddenSections.

    Can I change a phone number or email?

    Add the new one. The old one is kept as an earlier line with its date, not deleted.

    Who sees my register record?

    Only you, after sign-in.

      Developer notes

      1. Security and privacy

      1. Phone and WhatsApp stay masked exactly as on the live page (+855 12 869 •••), also in the exported JSON. Never put a full number in this file.
      2. Visibility tags decide what goes public: Public, Behind the check (masked until the visitor passes the security check), Only you, We fill. Only you and We fill never leave the owner page.
      3. This mockup sends nothing: it saves in the browser only. Production saves to the owner record after sign-in (owner-pass), and our agents check the change before it goes live.

      2. Money and ads

      1. No ads and no prices on this page. Prices only come from the owner, never from scraping.

      3. Layout

      1. Phone width first. One closed row per field; the top boxes open as cards. Fields follow x-priority in master-listing.schema.json; Known for (max 120 characters) is its own field, separate from the short description.
      2. "More about this field" shows the schema.org name (custom fields marked), the old CMS field names, or "NEW: no old field".
